There has never been a dull moment in history. Every year we see transformation
in our society and there is no way to stop it. People are always forced to
adapt and get used to new realities.

I remember the time when printing as a profession was being influenced by
computers. Printing itself was also a technological breakthrough. The Gutenberg
Press was an invention created at the end of the middle ages. The impact that
had on society was that it democratized access to text and human knowledge. AI
today, democratizes the creation of content and computer code.

During the industrial revolution, muscle power was exchanged with big machines
powered by steam. Administrative tasks and routine tasks are now being automated by
algorithms LLMs.

This is nothing new and just like the horse cart coupler repairman had to
become a mechanic, software devs must take on a new role.
